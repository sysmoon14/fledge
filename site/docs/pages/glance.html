The glance

When the Nest sees you come into the room, it can show the glance: the time, the weather and what's on. Walk up close, and it switches to your cards.

Turning it on

In the integration's Configure, or the panel's Wake & presence tab, choose what Across the room and Close up show. With a glance across the room, the Nest shows it when it lights up because someone walked in; a touch, or coming close, shows what it wakes to.

The Nest only sees someone across the room while its own Farsight is on (any of its choices) in Nest's settings. With Farsight off, it wakes only on a touch.

Designing it

The panel's Glance tab chooses what the glance shows, with a preview of the Nest's screen. Try on the Nest shows it for real for 20 seconds before you save.

Each layout keeps the lines you chose for it. The Glance layout select on the Nest Thermostat cards device switches layout from an automation.

Lines

Up to three lines under the big element. The Nest works out some itself: the date, the time, its temperature and humidity, its heating and time to temperature, the weather and what's on. Home Assistant sends the rest: today's high and low, when rain arrives, sunrise or sunset, what a speaker you choose is playing, your next event, who's home (first names), any entity's value (a door says Open, a timestamp reads as a time), or a template.

  • A line with nothing to say takes no room: the lines below move up. Or give it a fallback, such as now playing, else what's on.
  • An entity's line can show only while it matters: while it's on or off, in (or not in) a state, or above or below a number.
  • A long line scrolls.
  • A sensor that changes every second reaches the Nest at most every 15 seconds.

Clock and date

12 or 24 hours, or Automatic from where Home Assistant is, with or without AM/PM. The date can put the day or the month first, and uses Home Assistant's language for its words where that's written in the Latin alphabet.

Calendars

Up next and the next event's line look through the calendars you choose for the next event that isn't over, and can leave out all-day ones.

At night

A different layout and lines from sunset to sunrise (shifted by up to three hours), between set times, or while a sensor such as a bedtime switch is on (or off). It can be drawn dimmer and warmer, as dim as you choose. This dims what Fledge draws; the Nest keeps its own backlight level, which follows the room's light. The Night glance binary sensor is on while the night layout shows.

The glance's layouts need the thermostat on Fledge v0.9.0 or later; an older build shows the clock.
