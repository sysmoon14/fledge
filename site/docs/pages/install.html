Install on the thermostat

The Fledge app does it in one guided flow: connect the thermostat, point it at your MQTT broker, choose features, back up, patch.

Back up first. The app offers a whole-NAND backup before it patches (Back up, then patch, the default). Take it on the first run: it is the only way to put the thermostat back exactly as it was.

1. Get the app

Download it from the latest release, under Assets:

Your computerDownload
Mac (macOS 13 or later)Fledge-macos-universal-<version>.zip
WindowsFledge-windows-x86_64-<version>.zip, or -arm64
Linux (glibc 2.28 or later)Fledge-linux-x86_64-<version>.tar.gz, or -arm64, -riscv64

Or build it yourself: see BUILDING.md.

macOS

The app is signed ad hoc, not notarized, so macOS refuses it at first. Allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security, or run:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine Fledge.app

Linux

Run the app as your normal user, never with sudo. It needs a one-time udev rule for USB access. Keep the 70- prefix: the rule must run before systemd's 73-seat-late.rules.

sudo tee /etc/udev/rules.d/70-fledge.rules > /dev/null <<'EOF'
# i.MX ROM serial download, recovery U-Boot
ATTRS{idVendor}=="15a2", ATTRS{idProduct}=="0071", TAG+="uaccess", ENV{ID_MM_DEVICE_IGNORE}="1"
ATTRS{idVendor}=="18d1", ATTRS{idProduct}=="4ee0", TAG+="uaccess", ENV{ID_MM_DEVICE_IGNORE}="1"
# the recovery initramfs shell and the Nest application's console
ATTRS{idVendor}=="0525", ATTRS{idProduct}=="a4a7", TAG+="uaccess", ENV{ID_MM_DEVICE_IGNORE}="1"
ATTRS{idVendor}=="0525", ATTRS{idProduct}=="b4a4", TAG+="uaccess", ENV{ID_MM_DEVICE_IGNORE}="1"
ATTRS{idVendor}=="2464", ATTRS{idProduct}=="0030", TAG+="uaccess", ENV{ID_MM_DEVICE_IGNORE}="1"
ATTRS{idVendor}=="2464", ATTRS{idProduct}=="0031", TAG+="uaccess", ENV{ID_MM_DEVICE_IGNORE}="1"
EOF
sudo udevadm control --reload-rules && sudo udevadm trigger

Reconnect the thermostat afterwards if it was already plugged in. The app also needs a Wayland or X11 desktop with OpenGL or Vulkan drivers, xdg-desktop-portal with your desktop's backend (for the file picker), and libudev. Logs go to ~/.local/share/fledge/logs/.

Windows

If a run stops with ACM interface for 18d1:4ee0 did not appear, see Troubleshooting.

2. Connect the thermostat

Take the display off its base and plug the micro-USB cable into the port on the back of the display. Not the power-only USB port on a European stand or Heat Link base, and not a charge-only cable: those are the usual reasons the app sees nothing. Open the app and choose Set up.

3. Follow Set up

  1. Connect: the app finds the thermostat and says what it is. A generation Fledge doesn't support is recognised and left alone.
  2. Home Assistant: your MQTT broker's address, user and password (and a CA certificate for TLS if you use one), and whether the thermostat should take updates through Home Assistant. Leave updates on unless you have a reason not to; see Updates.
  3. Features:
    • Your heating: how the Nest switches your boiler. On a Heat Link model with no Heat Link (or with Zigbee or Thread on), answer Wired to the Nest's base, or no Heat Link, or the Nest stops with error H71 or H74.
    • Block cloud: cuts the thermostat off from Nest's and Google's servers.
    • Matter (over Wi-Fi, or Thread) and Zigbee (as a router). Thread and Zigbee share the thermostat's radio, so only one can be on, and on European models either one replaces the wireless Heat Link connection.
    • SSH, with a key the app can create for you. Password login stays off unless you allow it.
    • The USB console, and Doom. While Doom runs, the thermostat doesn't control the heating.
  4. Review: shows your choices and offers Back up, then patch. Developer options (dry run, slot, entry point and the like) are folded away here; you don't need them.

The run shows its steps in place. When it finishes, Done says what to do next. The thermostat reboots into Fledge; put it back on its base.

4. Wi-Fi and Home Assistant

If the thermostat isn't on Wi-Fi yet, join it from its own menu (Settings → Network). Once it reaches the broker, it appears in Home Assistant through MQTT discovery as a Nest Thermostat device with its climate entity and the rest; see Entities.

Now add the Fledge integration to put your devices on its screen.

On the thermostat

Fledge adds a page to the Nest's own Settings for what you turned on: Matter's pairing code and QR, Zigbee pairing, and Doom.

A few options can be changed later from Home Assistant without the app: the Nest's External temperature switch (see Room thermometer) and its Works With Nest boiler switch. Changing either reboots the thermostat into it.
