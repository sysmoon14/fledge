In testing: v0.10

These are in test releases (thermostat v0.10.0-rc, integration 0.19.0-rc), not yet released for everyone. They may change before they are.

To try them, install both test releases together: see Test releases. An older thermostat build shows the "thermostat outdated" repair once you use one of these, and draws a battery face as a clock.

Face cards

A card can be a glance face of its own, designed like the glance: add one to the cards in the Fledge panel. Each starts as a copy of the glance, and is its own from then on.

  • Its ring holds actions you choose, up to four after Back: scenes, scripts and buttons run; lights and switches turn on or off.
  • A hold can do one of those actions.

Ring limits

Each card with a ring can have a Lowest and Highest: a thermostat's temperature, a speaker's volume, a cover's position, a light's brightness and warmth. Blank goes as far as the device does. Set them in the panel.

  • The ring's ends are the limits: a full ring is your highest, an empty ring your lowest. The card says At your limit when you reach one.
  • The Nest's commands stay within them. Home Assistant and automations can still set anything.
  • A light group's limits hold for its lights too, unless a light has its own.
  • Can be pushed past, for each end: keep turning, three arrows light one a click, and the next click goes through. Past the limit, the ring stays full (or empty) and the arrows, number and words turn amber. A limit that isn't set to give way holds.
  • Opened while the value is already past a firm limit, the card waits at that end: the number shows grey, and it says how to turn back. The first turn back sets the limit.

The battery face

A glance layout for anything with a battery: a car, a home battery, a mower. Use it as the glance or as a face card.

  • The ring is the level: green while charging, amber and then red as it runs low, with a white notch at the charge limit. In the Nest look, charging fills the face green.
  • Choose its level entity, and optionally its charging, plugged-in, power and charge-limit entities. The line under the number is worked out from them (Charging · 7.2 kW, Plugged in), with one more line of your choice.
  • Give it an icon, or an image: a /local/ file, an image uploaded to Home Assistant, or a web address.
