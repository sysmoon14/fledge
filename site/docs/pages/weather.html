Weather and room temperature

Home Assistant can give the Nest its weather, a better thermometer, and keep count of how long the heating ran.

The Nest's weather

The weather entity you pick (in the panel's Thermostat tab, or the integration's options) feeds the Nest's own weather screen too: the outside temperature and humidity, and the forecast, every 15 minutes and whenever it changes. Pick an outside thermometer as well to use its temperature instead of the forecast's. This replaces the old Nest weather blueprint.

Room thermometer

The Nest can regulate from a Home Assistant temperature sensor instead of its own, in °C or °F. If the readings stop for 30 minutes, it goes back to its own.

This turns off the Nest's Bluetooth, so real Nest Temperature Sensors stop working. Turning it on or off reboots the Nest, so its heating pauses for about a minute.

The Nest needs Fledge's external temperature option for this. Turn it on in any of these ways:

  • the Nest's External temperature switch in Home Assistant;
  • the toggle in the panel's Weather and temperature;
  • the repair Home Assistant shows when you choose a room thermometer without it.

The Nest's Temperature source sensor says which thermometer it's using.

Heating and hot water hours

Heating today and Hot water today count the hours the Nest has called for each since midnight. They're kept as long-term statistics, so a statistics graph card shows them by day, week or month.

type: statistics-graph
entities:
  - sensor.nest_thermostat_cards_heating_today
stat_types: [change]
period: day
chart_type: bar

Check the sensor's entity ID on the Nest Thermostat cards device; yours may differ.
