The thermostat and Settings

The thermostat is a card like the others, drawn in the same style, and its everyday Settings come with it.

The thermostat card

Open it and turn to set the target temperature. Its options hold Eco, heating on or off and a hot water boost (where the Nest drives hot water). The card shows when it's heating and how long until it reaches the temperature.

The card can sit anywhere in the ring: drag it in the Fledge panel. Rename the thermostat's device in Home Assistant and the card takes the new name.

Fledge sets the thermostat through the same settings Home Assistant uses, and Nest's own software still runs the heating, the schedule, the learning and the safety limits.

Settings

The Settings card is always last. It holds the everyday settings, in the same style:

  • Eco temperature
  • Home/Away Assist, Auto-Schedule, Early-On, Sunblock
  • Units
  • Theme: Fledge's look or the Nest's
  • Nest's own dial, instead of Fledge's thermostat card
  • Fledge updates

Everything else is in Nest's own menus, a press away from Settings. Back in Nest's menu returns to the cards.

Nest's own dial

Prefer the Nest's original thermostat screen? Turn on Nest's own dial in Settings. The thermostat card then opens the Nest exactly as it always was.

As a panel only

Turn off the thermostat's Act as a thermostat switch in Home Assistant and it stops controlling heating: the screen shows only your cards. Turning it back on brings back its heating entities, which Home Assistant may re-create under new entity IDs; check any automations that use them.
