The thermostat and Settings

The thermostat is a card like the others, drawn in the same style, and its everyday Settings come with it.

The thermostat card

Open it and turn to set the target temperature. Its options hold Eco, heating on or off and a hot water boost (where the Nest drives hot water). The card shows when it's heating and how long until it reaches the temperature.

The card can sit anywhere in the ring: drag it in the Fledge panel. Rename the thermostat's device in Home Assistant and the card takes the new name.

Its ring can stop at a lowest and highest temperature you set on its card in the panel, each firm or able to be pushed past. See Ring limits. They need Fledge v0.10.1 or later on the thermostat.

Fledge sets the thermostat through the same settings Home Assistant uses, and Nest's own software still runs the heating, the schedule, the learning and the safety limits.

Settings

The Settings card is always last. It holds the everyday settings, in the same style:

  • Eco temperature
  • Home/Away Assist, Auto-Schedule, Early-On, Sunblock
  • Units
  • Theme: Fledge's look or the Nest's
  • Nest's own dial, instead of Fledge's thermostat card
  • Fledge updates

Everything else is in Nest's own menus, a press away from Settings. Back in Nest's menu returns to the cards.

Nest's own dial

Prefer the Nest's original thermostat screen? Turn on Nest's own dial in Settings. The thermostat card then opens the Nest exactly as it always was.

Home Assistant calls for heat

For homes where something else decides which rooms need heat (smart radiator valves, a zoning integration, an automation), the Nest can leave that decision to Home Assistant and only fire the boiler. It's off unless you turn it on, and changes nothing until you do.

  1. Turn on the thermostat's Heating control by Home Assistant switch.
  2. Its Call for heat switch (from the Fledge integration) becomes available. On asks the Nest to heat, off asks it not to. An automation can flip it, for example on while any radiator valve is heating, or use the fledge.call_for_heat action, which can also name the rooms asking.

While Home Assistant decides, the Nest's own schedule and Auto-Schedule wait aside. Asked for heat, it heats; asked for none, it's off. Its own safety temperature (which holds while it's off) and boiler cycle limits still apply.

Its card becomes a simple one: whether it's heating and why ("Office wants heat", "2 rooms want heat", "No rooms want heat"), and whether the hot water is on and until when. There's no temperature to set on it, as the rooms are set in Home Assistant. Press it for a hot water boost, or for Nest's own dial.

  • Call for heat is the only say. A temperature or mode set on Nest's own dial, or from the thermostat's climate entity in Home Assistant, is put back within seconds, and eco is cleared. To set the Nest by hand, turn heating control off.
  • If Home Assistant stops (a restart, the broker or Wi-Fi down), the asks stop. After ten minutes the Nest's own schedule comes back, at the temperature it sets for that time, and it runs as a normal Nest until Home Assistant asks again. With no schedule it holds 17°. A schedule you write meanwhile, from Home Assistant or the schedule card, is the one it comes back to.
  • Turning the switch off gives the Nest its schedule back straight away.

Heating only, for now: cooling and heat pumps carry on as before.

As a panel only

Turn off the thermostat's Act as a thermostat switch in Home Assistant and it stops controlling heating: the screen shows only your cards. Turning it back on brings back its heating entities, which Home Assistant may re-create under new entity IDs; check any automations that use them.
