Cards and the ring

Walk up and your devices show zoomed out around the thermostat. Turn to browse, press to open, and the ring becomes that device's control.

Nothing is switched or run from the chooser. A press there only ever opens a card, so you can't turn on a light by browsing past it. Once a card is open, a press shows its options and a hold does its main thing.

What you open stays open until you go back with Back in its options. Left alone, the screen times out (after 20 seconds by default, a camera after at least a minute), and the next wake starts again from the top.

Face cards

A card can be a glance face of its own: a clock, the weather, a big number, your next event or a battery, designed like the glance. Add one in the panel's Cards tab (Add a card, then A glance face) and design it on the Glance tab. Each starts as a copy of the glance, and is its own from then on.

  • Its options hold actions you choose, up to four after Back: scenes, scripts and buttons run; lights and switches turn on or off.
  • A hold can do one of those actions. Without one, a hold does nothing.

Ring limits

Each card with a ring can have a Lowest and Highest: a thermostat's temperature, a speaker's volume, a cover's position, a light's brightness and warmth. Leave them blank to go as far as the device does. Set them on the card in the panel's Cards tab.

  • The ring's ends are the limits: a full ring is your highest, an empty ring your lowest. The card says At your limit when you reach one.
  • The Nest's commands stay within them. Home Assistant, its app and your automations can still set anything.
  • A light group's limits hold for its lights too, unless a light has its own.
  • Can be pushed past, for each end: keep turning, three arrows light one a click, and the next click goes through. Past the limit the ring stays full (or empty), and the arrows, number and words turn amber.
  • A limit that can't be pushed past holds. Opened while the value is already beyond it, the card waits at that end: the number shows grey, and it says which way to turn. The first turn back sets the limit.

Two looks

Everything is drawn in Fledge's own look by default. The Nest's original look is one setting away: Settings → Theme on the thermostat. The Fledge panel in Home Assistant can preview either.

What the thermostat is allowed to do

Only to the devices you chose, and only: switch a light, switch or fan on or off; set a light's brightness, warmth or colour; set a thermostat's temperature; set a speaker's volume and play or pause it; run a scene, script or button (and a face card's actions); take a camera's picture; arm or disarm an alarm. Anything else it asks for is refused and logged. See Security.
