Wake and automations

The integration's Nest Thermostat cards device lets automations decide what the thermostat shows.

The cards device

EntityWhat it does
select Wake toWhat the thermostat shows as it wakes. See below.
switch Keep screen onKeeps the thermostat's display lit.
button Wake screenLights the display onto what it wakes to.
select Glance layoutClock, Temperature, Weather, Analog, Big number or Up next. See The glance.
binary_sensor Night glanceOn while the glance's night layout shows.

Wake to

  • Device chooser: all the cards, zoomed out.
  • Automatic: the speaker that's playing; else, after sunset, a light that's on; else the chooser.
  • The thermostat, as the Nest always did.
  • Any of your cards, by name.

Examples

Show the music while it plays

automation:
  - alias: Nest shows the music while it plays
    triggers:
      - trigger: state
        entity_id: media_player.kitchen_speaker
    actions:
      - action: select.select_option
        target:
          entity_id: select.nest_thermostat_cards_wake_to
        data:
          option: "{{ 'Kitchen speaker' if is_state('media_player.kitchen_speaker', 'playing') else 'Device chooser' }}"

Keep the screen on while you cook

automation:
  - alias: Nest stays on while the oven is on
    triggers:
      - trigger: state
        entity_id: switch.oven
    actions:
      - action: "switch.turn_{{ trigger.to_state.state }}"
        target:
          entity_id: switch.nest_thermostat_cards_keep_screen_on

Wake to the lights at sunset

automation:
  - alias: Nest wakes to the lights after dark
    triggers:
      - trigger: sun
        event: sunset
      - trigger: sun
        event: sunrise
    actions:
      - action: select.select_option
        target:
          entity_id: select.nest_thermostat_cards_wake_to
        data:
          option: "{{ 'Living room lights' if trigger.event == 'sunset' else 'Device chooser' }}"

Entity IDs and card names here are examples; use your own. A card's option in Wake to is the name it has on the Nest.

For messages on the screen, see Notifications.
