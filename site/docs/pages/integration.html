Add it to Home Assistant

The Fledge integration picks which of your devices appear on the thermostat, in what order, and what it wakes to.

You need the thermostat running Fledge with MQTT, showing in Home Assistant as a Nest Thermostat device (Install on the thermostat), and Home Assistant 2026.3 or later.

1. Download it

Open the repository in Home Assistant's Marketplace (HACS) with this link, download Fledge, and restart Home Assistant:

Open Fledge in the Marketplace

Or add https://github.com/sysmoon14/fledge-homeassistant there by hand, as a custom repository of type Integration.

2. Set it up

After the restart, Home Assistant offers the thermostat under Settings → Devices & services. Or start it with this link:

Add the Fledge integration

Pick your devices, in the order you want them round the ring, and what the thermostat wakes to. Cards can be lights (a light group makes a whole room one card), switches and fans, other thermostats, speakers, selects, covers, scenes, scripts and buttons, cameras and alarm panels. See Cards and the ring for what each one does.

3. Arrange it in the panel

From then on, use Fledge in the sidebar (admins only) to change the cards: drag them into order, rename them for the Nest, and see each one drawn as the thermostat will draw it. See The Fledge panel. The integration's Configure button still works too.

What it adds

  • A Nest Thermostat cards device, connected to the thermostat's own, with Wake to, Keep screen on, Wake screen, Glance layout and Night glance. See Wake and automations.
  • The actions fledge.notify, fledge.dismiss and fledge.set_schedule. See Actions.
  • Two dashboard cards for the Nest's heating and hot water schedules. See Schedules.
  • Heating today and Hot water today sensors, and the weather feed for the Nest's own weather screen. See Weather and room temperature.

Matching versions

The integration and the thermostat's software are released apart. The thermostat says which version of the cards it understands; if the integration needs something newer, a repair under Settings → Repairs asks you to update the thermostat. See Updates.

Removing it

Removing the integration puts the thermostat's screen back as it was. The thermostat stays in Home Assistant through MQTT.
