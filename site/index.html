Your Nest thermostat, out of the nest.

Fledge roots a Nest Learning Thermostat over USB, cuts it loose from Google's cloud, and puts Home Assistant on its own screen.

The ring runs the house.

Walk up, and your devices show around the thermostat. Turn to browse, press to open, and the ring becomes that device's control, with the Nest's own clicks.

Still a Nest.

Nest's own software keeps running the heating, the schedule, the learning and the safety limits. Fledge runs beside it and stands in for Google's cloud, so the thermostat talks to Home Assistant over MQTT on your own network. No Google account, and no internet needed.

A glance from across the room

The time, the weather and what's on, when the Nest sees you come in. Your cards when you walk up.

Notifications

An automation can put a message or a camera's picture on the screen, and act on the press that answers it.

Updates from Home Assistant

Signed releases arrive as an ordinary update entity, and roll back by themselves if one fails to start.

Schedules on your dashboards

Two dashboard cards edit the Nest's own heating and hot water schedules.

Set-up is one guided flow.

  1. Plug the display into your computer

    Take it off its base and connect it with a micro-USB cable that carries data. The Fledge app runs on macOS, Windows and Linux.

  2. Follow Set up in the app

    Give it your MQTT broker and choose what you want. It offers a full backup of the thermostat first, so you can always put it back exactly as it was.

  3. Add the integration in Home Assistant

    The thermostat appears by itself. The Fledge integration puts your devices on its screen, in the order you drag them into.

Read this before you start

Fledge has been tested on exactly one thermostat: a 3rd generation Nest Learning Thermostat on Nest firmware 6.4-8, a European model with a Heat Link driving heating and hot water, set up with MQTT and Home Assistant. On anything else, you are the first.

It rewrites the flash of the device that runs your heating. A failed run can leave the thermostat unbootable. Take the backup, and have another way to heat the house while you try it. Fledge comes without any warranty.
